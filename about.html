About Me

I am currently a student at High Point University, pursuing a degree in Computer Science with a concentration in Cybersecurity. I chose this field because of the ever-evolving challenge of digital defense and the critical importance of protecting information in our interconnected world.

My background is uniquely international. I was born in Switzerland and have lived all over the world. This global upbringing sparked a deep passion for traveling, leading me to explore over 50 countries. This perspective allows me to adapt quickly to new environments, a skill vital in technology.

In my spare time, I stay active through sports and outdoor adventures. You can often find me on the soccer field or heading to the mountains for a weekend of skiing. I enjoy the focus and discipline required to succeed in both my hobbies and my career.
